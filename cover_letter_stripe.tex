This hands-on experience designing LLM-powered systems — including prompt engineering, tool-calling patterns, and multi-modal interactions — maps directly to the kind of work your Collaboration team is pursuing.

On the full-stack side, my experience aligns closely with what you are looking for. At \textbf{Cyberfox}, I architected a multi-product SaaS platform from the ground up using \textbf{TypeScript, React, and NestJS}, optimising backend performance for thousands of concurrent requests and leading the full SDLC from discovery to production. I am comfortable owning the entire vertical — from UX and API design through to infrastructure reliability — and have a track record of shipping robust, maintainable systems under real product pressure.

I also bring experience mentoring junior and mid-level engineers, having run peer code reviews and technical workshops at Hexacta. I understand that a small, nimble team like yours depends on strong cross-functional communication and autonomous decision-making, both of which I have honed across distributed, cross-timezone Agile environments.

While my primary expertise is in TypeScript and Node.js, I am a fast learner with experience across multiple languages and runtimes, and I am confident I will get up to speed with Ruby quickly. More broadly, I thrive in environments that demand engineering rigour while experimenting with emerging technologies — which is exactly the balance your team appears to strike.

I would love the opportunity to discuss how my background aligns with your team's goals. Thank you for your time and consideration.
